To link a billing system to your MikroTik RB951, you need to configure your router to communicate with external software using RADIUS authentication or the MikroTik API. [1, 2] 
## 1. Choose Your Billing Method

* Hotspot Vouchers / Pay-per-use: Best for public Wi-Fi, cafes, or hostels where users log into a captive portal page.
* PPPoE Subscriptions: Best for home or office internet plans where users connect via a dialed-in username and password.
* Popular Billing Platforms: Third-party software options like Mikhmon, Centipid Billing, Pawa WiFi, or SopraEnt handle customer management, packages, and automated payment gateways (such as mobile money or card payments). [2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7] 

## 2. General Steps to Link Your MikroTik RB951

   1. Prepare the Router: Connect your MikroTik to the internet and make sure it has a stable time configuration (NTP client) so session times match your billing logs. [1, 8] 
   2. Configure Hotspot or PPPoE: Set up the basic Hotspot or PPPoE service on the correct local/wireless interface of your RB951 via WinBox. [1, 8] 
   3. Set up RADIUS Client: In MikroTik RouterOS, navigate to RADIUS, add a new server, and input your billing provider's server IP address and shared secret password. Enable hotspot and/or ppp services under the RADIUS settings. [1, 8] 
   4. Connect the Payment Gateway / API: Link your billing dashboard to your payment provider (e.g., M-Pesa or local mobile money APIs). When a customer pays, the billing platform uses the RADIUS or MikroTik API connection to instantly create, provision, and assign speed limits to their account. [1, 6, 9]

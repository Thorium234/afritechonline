Yes, you can absolutely use WinBox to connect to your MikroTik CHR (Cloud Hosted Router) inside your virtual machine, and you can connect that same CHR to your billing system.
In fact, using WinBox to configure your virtual CHR is the exact industry-standard way to manage it, just like you would manage a physical RB951.
To make sure your WinBox, your CHR (Virtual Machine), and your Billing System (Code/Application) can all talk to each other simultaneously, you need to set up the virtual network adapters correctly.
## 1. How to Configure Your Virtual Machine (VirtualBox Example)
Before turning on the CHR virtual machine, open its settings and configure two network adapters:

* Adapter 1: Bridged Adapter (or NAT)
* Purpose: Connects your CHR to your actual physical router/home internet.
   * Result: This allows the CHR to pull the correct time via NTP and communicate out to payment gateway APIs.
* Adapter 2: Host-Only Adapter
* Purpose: Creates a private, virtual network switch inside your computer that only your main laptop OS and your Virtual Machines can see.
   * Result: Your WinBox and your Billing System application will use this specific virtual network to securely send API and RADIUS commands to the CHR.

## 2. Steps to Connect via WinBox

   1. Launch your VirtualBox/VMware and start the MikroTik CHR.
   2. Once it boots to the command-line login prompt, open WinBox on your laptop.
   3. Click on the Neighbors tab at the bottom.
   4. You will see your CHR appear in the list showing its virtual MAC address and IP address.
   5. Click on the MAC address, enter the default login (Login: admin, Password: leave blank or what you set during initialization), and click Connect.

## 3. Enabling the Connection to Your System
Once you are inside WinBox, you must enable the specific "ports" so your billing system can talk to the CHR over the Host-Only network.
In WinBox, go to IP -> Services:

* For API Billing Systems: Ensure api (port 8728) or api-ssl (port 8729) is enabled.
* For Web Portal Management: Ensure www (port 80) or winbox (port 8291) are active if your system needs to redirect users.
